# Demonstrations

Worked analyses built on the project's databases — each published with its data, its code, its category definitions, and its limits, so the analysis can be checked rather than taken on faith.

**Browse:** https://beperron.github.io/SocialWork-MetaData/demonstrations/

| Demonstration | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Artificial Intelligence in Social Work, 1989–2026](ai-in-social-work/) | 278 verified articles across thirty-seven years: growth, publication outlets, and what the literature actually does (empirical / reception / commentary) in each technological era. Includes the embedding-based recall audit and the full labeled corpus. |

Each demonstration corresponds to a [Cookbook](../cookbook/) recipe — the copy-paste prompt that produces the same kind of analysis on a topic of your choosing.

## Conventions

- **Data is released.** Every demonstration publishes the records it analyzed, including the ones it rejected, so a reader can re-screen rather than argue in the abstract.
- **Code is runnable.** Retrieval, statistics, and figures are scripted. Screening and labeling are not — those require reading, and the demonstrations say so.
- **Sources stay separate.** Where a versioned database release is extended with an external source (e.g. Web of Science), the supplement is stored and reported separately, never merged into the database records.
- **Limits are stated in the text,** not buried: journal-set boundaries, indexing lag, abstract-level coding, and any contestable boundary rule that moves the counts.
