Artificial Intelligence in Social Work

Every article on AI in the disciplinary social work journals across thirty-seven years, verified by reading and classified by what each study does. Shows that the field's engagement with AI began with expert systems in 1989 — long before generative chatbots — and traces how the balance shifted from arguing about the technology, to building it, to studying how practitioners respond to it.
